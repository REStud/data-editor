How this counts. A word is any run of characters separated by white space — the same definition AEA applies when it counts equations, footnotes, and endnotes as part of the body text. Two text runs with no real gap between them (a kerned heading, a logo) are treated as one word rather than split apart, which is where a naive PDF text count tends to inflate. Even so, expect this to land within about a percentage point of AEA's own tool, not match it exactly — their converter has internal normalization this can't see. REStud has not published its own counting rule, so treat the limits above as guidance, not a guarantee the editorial office will count identically. Title, author names, abstract, acknowledgement footnote, references, and exhibits are conventionally excluded from AEA-style counts. The notes column is a hint from a keyword search, not a determination — the checkbox decides what is actually counted.

Insights: 6,000 words / 5 exhibits · Communications: 2,000 words / 2 exhibits, per restud.com. Modeled on the AEA word-count tool.
