Since Jan 1, 2026, the Data Editor of the Review of Economic Studies is [Andrea Moro](https://andreamoro.net) (Vanderbilt University), who can be reached at [restud_dataeditor@andreamoro.net](mailto:restud_dataeditor@andreamoro.net)

The first data editor of the Review was [Miklós Koren](https://koren.mk/), who served in this capacity from Jan 1, 2019 to Dec 31, 2025.

The Review of Economic Studies had a data-and-code availability policy since 2006.
